Dwarkadhish Temple

Architectural Marvel of Mathura

Built in 1814, the Dwarkadhish Temple is one of the most visited shrines in Mathura. It is famous for its striking architecture, beautiful ceiling paintings depicting Lord Krishna's life, and a grand black marble idol of the deity.

Key Highlights:
  • Beautiful Maratha-styled art and carvings
  • Lively Hindola festival during the monsoon season
  • Located right near the bustling ghats of the Yamuna River
