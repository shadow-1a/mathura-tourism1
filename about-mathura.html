About Mathura

The Sacred Cultural Core of Braj Bhoomi

Historical Significance

Mathura is an ancient, historically prominent city located along the banks of the sacred Yamuna River in Uttar Pradesh, India. Renowned globally as the birthplace of Lord Krishna, it serves as the spiritual heart of the Braj region.

Grand Vrindavan-Mathura Shrine
Sacred Bathing Ghats

It stands as one of India's oldest continuously inhabited cities, boasting architectural and cultural heritage spanning thousands of years through early empires like the Kushan era.

Shri Krishna Devotion

The entire identity of Mathura is beautifully intertwined with the stories and pastimes of Lord Krishna. Millions of pilgrims travel here annually to experience the divine vibrations of the land where He spent His early childhood years.

Traditional Krishna Painting
Festive Temple Darshan

From the rhythmic morning prayers to the grand midnight celebrations during Janmashtami, the atmosphere resonates with deep spiritual energy, music, and love.

Vibrant Living Culture

The city thrives on unique traditional dynamic expressions, including continuous temple bhajans, traditional Sanjhi art forms, and rhythmic Raslila dance dramas recreating mystical spiritual history.
