Kansa Qila

An Ancient Riverside Fort

Named after Lord Krishna's maternal uncle, King Kansa, this historic fort stands as a silent witness to the ancient eras. It features a unique structural blend of early Hindu styles and later Islamic renovations by Raja Man Singh of Amber.

Key Highlights:
  • Massive stone fort walls overlooking the Yamuna
  • Historic observatory ruins built by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh
  • Great spot for architecture students and history enthusiasts
