Govardhan Hill

The Sacred Hillside of Protection

Govardhan Hill holds deep spiritual significance. According to legends, Lord Krishna lifted this massive hill on his little finger to shield the people of Braj from the devastating storms sent by Lord Indra.

Key Highlights:
  • The 21-kilometer Parikrama (circumambulation) route walked by pilgrims
  • Radha Kund and Shyam Kund nearby
  • Massive crowds during the Govardhan Puja festival
