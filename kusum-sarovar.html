Kusum Sarovar

The Peaceful Royal Reservoir

Kusum Sarovar is a beautiful 450-foot-long sandstone lake surrounded by striking historic structures erected by the rulers of Bharatpur. Folklore states this is where Radha used to collect flowers.

Key Highlights:
  • Stunning reflection of the sandstone monuments in the water
  • Calm, quiet environment away from traffic noise
  • Perfect location for landscape photography
